Help & FAQ

Find answers to commonly asked questions about FreeResume AI.

Getting Started

How do I create a resume?

From the dashboard, tap the "+" button to create a new resume. You'll be guided through adding your personal information, work experience, education, and skills.

Do I need to create an account?

No, you can use FreeResume AI without creating an account. However, creating an account allows you to sync your resumes across multiple devices.

Is the app really free?

Yes! You can create resumes, use templates, and export to PDF completely free. We show occasional ads to support the free service.

Templates

What templates are available?

  • ATS-Optimized: Designed to pass Applicant Tracking Systems
  • Professional: Clean, corporate-friendly designs
  • Modern: Contemporary layouts with visual appeal
  • Creative: Bold designs for creative industries
  • Minimal: Simple, elegant formatting

Can I change templates after creating a resume?

Yes! You can change your template at any time from the Export screen.

Export & Sharing

How do I download my resume?

From the Preview screen, tap the "Download PDF" button. If an ad is available, you can watch a short video to download for free.

What paper sizes are supported?

We support US Letter (8.5" x 11") and A4 (210 x 297mm).
